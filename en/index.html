What appears in each new tab?

An entity or a headline to discover what is happening, with context and links to the sources. You choose where to go next.

  1. Can I choose what I see?

    Yes. Open the three-line menu and choose Entities or Headlines, and the Spain or US edition. Your preferences are saved for future tabs.

  2. Where can I read the full story?

    Select the headline to open the article at its original source. Lootura gives you a starting point; the stories and bylines belong to their publishers.

  3. What if something big happens?

    Special coverage appears in red and brings together the entities involved. You can follow it or continue with your usual selection.

  4. Why does a story say “From the archive”?

    When recent coverage is scarce, you can discover older stories with their original dates. If you go offline, the saved copy lets you keep reading. Select Refresh at the bottom of the menu to check for updates for your next tab.

    Find the official store link here when the version for your browser is ready.
